# Code of Conduct

Sentinel welcomes contributors with different backgrounds, experience levels, and perspectives. This code applies to issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project spaces.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Discuss ideas and evidence; avoid personal attacks.
- Accept reasonable feedback and correct mistakes.
- Respect people's privacy and boundaries.
- Make participation accessible to newcomers.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory or sexualized language, targeted insults, and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable. Do not post credentials or sensitive log data.

## Moderation and reporting

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, or restrict participation when necessary. Responses should consider the impact, severity, and whether behavior is repeated.

For project moderation questions, open an issue without sensitive details or personal accusations. For harassment or other violations of GitHub's platform rules, use GitHub's Report abuse tools. Do not publish someone else's private information when reporting a concern. Security vulnerabilities have a separate private reporting process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
